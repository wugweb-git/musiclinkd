# Brand

Keep brand materials simple and reusable.

## Core identity

- **Name:** MusicLinkd
- **Positioning:** The professional network for music.
- **Voice:** Clear, confident, creative, and professional. Avoid hype and generic marketplace language.
- **Primary colour:** `#EA4C89`
- **Primary dark:** `#0D0B26`
- **Type:** Inter Tight for display; Inter for body.

## Asset rules

- Put approved logos, photos, social templates, and campaign artwork in `assets/`.
- Use real artist and business photography rather than initials, emoji, or illustrated avatars.
- Keep source assets and exported delivery assets clearly named.
