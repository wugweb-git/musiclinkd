The Problem

The music industry still runs on word-of-mouth and WhatsApp groups.

Fragmented tools, scattered identities and manual processes leave professionals wasting time on logistics instead of music.

01

Collaborators are impossible to find

Musicians still rely on random Instagram comments, Facebook groups and WhatsApp to find the right singer, producer or session player — with no way to verify credentials.

02

Studios live on word-of-mouth

There is no trusted directory of studios with verified equipment, real pricing and honest reviews. Booking happens over fragmented calls and texts.

03

Portfolios are scattered

An artist's real work lives across YouTube, Google Drive, Instagram, SoundCloud and private links. There is no single place that proves what you've actually done.

04

Professional credibility is unverified

Without a trusted identity layer, anyone can claim anything. Labels, studios and collaborators have no fast way to confirm who someone really is.

Trust Engine

Built around trust.
Not algorithms.

Every profile, studio, review and credit is verifiable. Because in music, reputation is everything.

Verified Professionals

Credits verified by multiple parties. No more unverified bio claims.

Verified Studios

Equipment, pricing and availability are verified and kept current.

Verified Reviews

Only real collaborators can review. No anonymous ratings.

Credits Ledger

A permanent, multi-party verified record of every release credit.

How the Credits Trust Engine works

When someone claims a credit on a release, all other credited parties are invited to verify. Once verified, the credit becomes a permanent, trusted part of that professional's identity.

Design Philosophy

Designed for people, not dashboards.

Every pixel is built around the real ways music professionals work, connect and create.

Clarity over complexity

MusicLinkd strips away the noise. We use large typography, generous whitespace, and deliberate hierarchy so what matters — people, projects, and opportunities — stays front and center.

No crowded dashboards. No blinking metrics. Just a clean, editorial experience that puts music professionals first.

01

Typography-first

Inter Tight for display creates a modern, editorial feel. Bold sizes and tight letter-spacing establish clear information hierarchy without decoration.

02

Device-native

Every interface component is designed at 360dp and scales up. The mobile experience drives every design decision.

03

Trust as UI

Verification badges, credit ledgers and review systems are surfaced directly in the interface — not hidden in settings.
