28 / Design Principles

Design Principles

Principle Description
Discovery-First Browse before commit. Audio-first portfolios.
People-First Real photos, real names, verified credits.
Trust by Design Multi-party verification, escrow payments.
Mobile-First 360dp viewport, touch targets 48dp min.
Audio-First Waveform previews on every card.
Editorial Generous spacing, clear hierarchy.

Design Philosophy

MusicLinkd is a professional network for music — not a social feed. Every interaction should feel like walking into a well-curated studio: warm, professional, and built for serious work.

Principle Rule
Clarity over cleverness Plain language, obvious actions
Audio over text Always show waveform previews
Verified over claimed Credits engine, not bio claims
Generous over cramped 64px section padding, 24px card padding
Pink for action #EA4C89 only on CTAs and active states

Success Celebratory, brief "Booking confirmed! 🎉"
Trust Authoritative, transparent "Verified by 3 collaborators"
