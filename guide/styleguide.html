Design Philosophy Stable v4.0

MusicLinkd is a professional network for music — not a social feed. Every interaction should feel like walking into a well-curated studio: warm, professional, and built for serious work.

PrincipleRule
Clarity over clevernessPlain language, obvious actions
Audio over textAlways show waveform previews
Verified over claimedCredits engine, not bio claims
Generous over cramped64px section padding, 24px card padding
Pink for action#EA4C89 only on CTAs and active states

Platform Modules 10 Modules

The 10 core product modules in MusicLinkd's full-stack music industry ecosystem. Each module maps to a Jetpack Compose feature package in app/features/ and a navigation destination in AppNavigationGraph.kt.

01 Discovery Feed
Swipe-deck + scrollable artist/studio cards. AI-ranked feed. Filters by role, genre, city.
DiscoverFeature.kt
02 Professional Profiles
Verified bio, audio portfolio, credit ledger, role badges, genre chips, availability status.
ProfileFeature.kt
03 Studio Booking
Real-time slot calendar, room + equipment selection, hourly pricing, escrow confirmation.
StudioBookingFeature.kt
04 Marketplace
Buy / sell / rent music gear between verified professionals. Category filters, price, condition, city.
MarketplaceFeature.kt
05 Messaging & Collaboration
Real-time Firebase chat, file sharing, project rooms, in-app session scheduling.
MessagingFeature.kt
06 Credits Trust Engine
Multi-party release credit verification. Verified credits become a permanent, tamper-proof professional record.
Verified Credits
07 Reviews & Reputation
Role-specific verified reviews — only real collaborators can review. Studio, artist, and gear reviews.
Verified Reviews
08 AI Matchmaking
Gemini 2.5 Flash synergy engine. Matches by genre, skill, availability, project history & compatibility score.
GeminiRecommendationEngine.kt
09 Opportunities & Briefs
Labels and businesses post project briefs. Artists and studios apply. AI pre-filters matches.
Opportunities Feed
10 Analytics & Insights
Profile views, play counts, connection rate, booking conversion, and AI-powered profile optimiser.
AnalyticsHelper.kt
