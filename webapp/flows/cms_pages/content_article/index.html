What a session rate should actually cover

Published 28 August 2026 · 6 min read

Most people quote for the hours they will be in the room. That is the part everyone remembers and the smallest part of the work.

A four-hour tracking session is rarely four hours. There is the listening you do beforehand, the charts or the lyric you prepare, the travel, and the revisions afterwards that nobody wrote down. Quoting only for the room means the rest is free.

What to include

PrepUsually 1–2 hours
The sessionBooked hours
RevisionsSay how many are included
TravelOutside your city, at cost

The second thing people forget is the deposit. Half up front is normal here, and it is the difference between a booking and an intention.

Frequently asked

When is the deposit taken?

When the studio confirms your slot, not when you request it. If they decline, nothing is charged.

Can I cancel a booking?

Free up to 48 hours before. Inside 48 hours the studio keeps the deposit.

When do I get paid?

Within seven days of a completed session, to the account on your profile.

Why was my listing removed?

Usually a prohibited item or someone else's photos. You always get an email saying which.

Marketplace rules

Effective14 August 2026
Version2.1

What you may list

Only what you own and can hand over or ship. No counterfeits, no stolen gear, no listings for something you have not got yet.

Offers

An offer is binding for 48 hours. A seller may counter once. Neither side is charged until an offer is accepted.

Deposits and disputes

Rental deposits are held, not paid out, until the item comes back. If the two sides disagree we hold the deposit until both have been heard.

Removal

We remove listings that break these rules and email you the reason. Repeated breaches end an account.
