Two decisions worth making before your profile goes live. Both are in Settings afterwards.

Who can find you

Anyone You appear in Discover and in search engines. Most people want this.
MusicLinkd members Signed-in people only.
Only people I share my link with You won't be discovered at all.

Who can message you

Anyone Work often arrives from someone you don't know yet.
Connections only Quieter, and you'll miss cold enquiries.

Your exact address is never shown — only your city. Your phone and email stay private unless you choose otherwise.
