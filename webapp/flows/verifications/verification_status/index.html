Why verify?

People book strangers on this platform. A badge tells them we checked something concrete — not that we recommend you, and not that your work is good.

What happens to my documents?

They are encrypted, seen only by the person reviewing them, and deleted once the check is done. They are never shown on your profile. Only the status — verified or not — is public.

How long does it take?

Usually two working days. We won't promise faster than we can deliver.

What if I'm rejected?

You get a reason, and you fix only the thing that was wrong — you don't start again. There's no limit on how many times you can try.
