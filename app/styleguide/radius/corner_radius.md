# Styleguide — Corner Radius System

## Radius Tokens

- `RadiusContainer`: `24.dp` — Mandatory corner radius for all cards, dialogs, and sheets.
- `RadiusPill`: `999.dp` — Buttons, genre chips, and search input pills.
- `RadiusThumbnail`: `12.dp` to `16.dp` — Media thumbnails and album art preview cards.
