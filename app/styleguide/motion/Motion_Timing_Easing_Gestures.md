# Styleguide — Motion Tokens, Easing & Touch Gestures

## Motion Timing Tokens
- `DurationFast`: `150.ms` — Button press ripples and checkmark icon toggles.
- `DurationMedium`: `300.ms` — Tab switching crossfades and filter chip transitions.
- `DurationSlow`: `500.ms` — Bottom sheet expand/collapse and modal entrance.

## Easing Curves
- `FastOutSlowInEasing`: Standard Material 3 decelerate curve for entrance transitions.
- `LinearOutSlowInEasing`: Exit transitions for dismissed banners.

## Gestures & Interactions
- **Swipe-to-Dismiss:** Horizontal swipe gesture on direct message notifications.
- **Pinch-to-Zoom:** Photo gallery modal inspection.
- **Press & Hold:** Waveform audio preview scrub gesture.
