# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — MusicLinkd

## 1. Executive Summary
MusicLinkd (`app.musiclinkd.com`) is a dedicated professional networking platform and marketplace designed for independent musicians, producers, session vocalists, audio engineers, recording studios, and indie record labels.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Independent Artists & Bands:** Looking for session collaborators, producers, or live gig venues.
- **Session Vocalists & Instrumentalists:** Monetizing specialized talent and stem contributions.
- **Recording Studios & Mix Engineers:** Selling studio time, mastering services, and equipment rentals.
- **Indie Record Labels & A&R:** Discovering verified local talent and managing open audition calls.

## 3. Key Value Propositions
- **Unified Creative Portfolios:** Waveform STEM audio previews, gear lists, verified credits, and client reviews.
- **Real-Time Collaboration Engine:** Connection requests, direct messaging, and studio session bookings.
- **AI Matchmaking:** Gemini 1.5 Flash collaborator synergy scoring based on genres, location, and creative goals.
- **Deep Linking & Mobile Web Support:** Shareable links at `app.musiclinkd.com` and `musiclinkd.page.link`.

## 4. Key Metrics for Success
- Connection request acceptance rate (> 75%)
- Audio STEM preview play rate
- Studio booking conversion
- Monthly Active Network Users (MANU)
