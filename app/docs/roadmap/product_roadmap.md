# Product Roadmap — MusicLinkd

## Milestone Goals

### Q3 2026 (v1.3.0 - Current)
- Complete design system & documentation suite.
- Enforce explicit product separation (`musiclinkd.com`, `app.musiclinkd.com`, `admin.musiclinkd.com`).
- Refactor screen components into modular sub-packages.

### Q4 2026 (v1.4.0 - Planned)
- Live WebRTC audio jam session rooms.
- In-app payment processing for studio slot bookings.
