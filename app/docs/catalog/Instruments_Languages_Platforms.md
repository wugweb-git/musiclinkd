## 🎸 Supported Musical Instruments & Specializations

Artists on `app.musiclinkd.com` can tag primary and secondary instruments in their verified portfolios:

### 1. Strings & Fretted
- Acoustic Guitar, Electric Guitar, Bass Guitar, Sitar, Sarod, Ukulele, Banjo, Mandolin, Harp, Cello, Violin, Viola, Double Bass.

### 2. Percussion & Rhythm
- Acoustic Drum Kit, Electronic Drum Pads, Tabla, Dholak, Cajón, Bongos, Congas, Ghatam, Kanjira, Timpani, Marimba.

### 3. Keyboards & Synthesizers
- Grand Piano, Electric Piano (Fender Rhodes / Wurlitzer), Analog Synthesizers, MIDI Controllers, Harmonium, Accordion, Organ.

### 4. Wind & Brass
- Bamboo Flute (Bansuri), Concert Flute, Saxophone (Alto / Tenor / Soprano), Trumpet, Trombone, Clarinet, Oboe, Harmonica, Shehnai.

### 5. Vocals & Top-Line
- Classical Lead Vocalist (Carnatic / Hindustani), Western Lead Vocalist, Session Backing Vocalist, Rapper / MC, Beatboxer, Top-line Songwriter.

## 🔗 External Supporting Platforms & Streaming Link Integration

Artists and studios can attach verified links to external streaming, social, and portfolio platforms across their profile cards:

| Platform | URL Pattern / Deep Link Standard | Supported Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spotify** | `https://open.spotify.com/artist/{id}` | Embed Top Tracks, Audio Snippets, Discography |
| **Apple Music** | `https://music.apple.com/artist/{id}` | Verified Discography & Album Previews |
| **YouTube Music** | `https://music.youtube.com/channel/{id}` | Official Music Video Embeds & Live Sets |
| **SoundCloud** | `https://soundcloud.com/{artist_handle}` | Raw Waveform Audio Previews & Unreleased STEMs |
| **Tidal** | `https://tidal.com/artist/{id}` | Hi-Fi Lossless Master Audio Previews |
| **Audiomack** | `https://audiomack.com/{artist_handle}` | Indie Mixtape & Release Analytics |
| **Bandcamp** | `https://{artist}.bandcamp.com` | Direct Merch & STEM Pack Sales |
| **Instagram** | `https://instagram.com/{handle}` | Behind-the-Scenes & Reels Highlights |
| **YouTube** | `https://youtube.com/@{channel}` | Studio Live Sessions & Vlogs |
